A first homeopathy consultation at Newport Homeopathy Clinic lasts 30–90 minutes and costs £50. It is a private, one-to-one conversation with Cavita Batchu, BHMS, either at 75 Forton Road, Newport or by video call anywhere in the UK.

## Before you book

If you're not sure whether a consultation is right for you, you can call or WhatsApp for a free, no-obligation chat first. When you're ready, [book a consultation](/book-appointment/).

## What to bring

- A list of any medicines and supplements you take
- Notes on your general health history, sleep, diet and routine
- Any questions you'd like to ask

## What happens during the consultation

Cavita takes time to understand your health, lifestyle and wellbeing as a whole, so that any advice is personal to you. You can ask questions at any point.

## After the first appointment

Follow-up appointments usually happen every 4–8 weeks and cost £30. You can have them [in person in Newport](/homeopathy-consultations/) or [online](/online-homeopathy-consultation/). See the full [fees](/consultation-fees/).

## Keep seeing your GP

Homeopathy is a complementary therapy. It is not a substitute for diagnosis or treatment by a medical professional. Please keep seeing your GP about any health concern, and do not stop or change prescribed medication without medical advice.
